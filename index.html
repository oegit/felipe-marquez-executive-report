Job description

In short: lead the team's creative quality and production while developing and implementing AI solutions that make the operation faster, more efficient and more scalable.

1. Art direction and creative production

  • Ensure the visual quality of all creative materials.
  • Oversee production of pieces for web, social media, email, video and sales.
  • Lead the design and video team.

  • Maintain brand consistency across all channels.

  • Create and maintain brand guidelines, templates and design systems.

2. AI development and automation

  • Identify opportunities to automate creative processes with AI.
  • Design and build AI production workflows.
  • Create AI agents for repetitive tasks.
  • Develop prompts, models and AI tools for design and video.
  • Integrate tools to automate processes across teams.
  • Implement quality, brand and privacy controls in the use of AI.
  • Research, test and recommend new AI tools.
  • Measure the time and cost savings generated by automation.
  • Document processes and train the team in the use of AI.
